01. Introduction

Welcome to FitTrack ("we," "our," or "us"). FitTrack is a personal fitness companion, workout planner, body measurement, and health tracking mobile application available on Android and iOS.

We believe your personal health and body measurements belong to you. This Privacy Policy outlines what information the application collects, how it is handled, and your choices regarding your data in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies, Apple App Store Review Guidelines, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

02. Information We Collect

FitTrack is designed to minimize data collection. We categorize data into two types:

Data Category Examples Storage Location
Health & Body Data Weight logs, BMI, body measurements (chest, waist, arms, thighs, hips, neck), daily calories, protein, carbs, water intake. Local device only (SQLite database)
Workout & Plans Custom workout routines, weekly schedule, completed sets, favorites. Local device only
App Preferences Dark/Light theme, metric/imperial units (cm/inches, kg/lbs), preferred language. Local device only (SharedPreferences)
Technical Diagnostics Crash stack traces, device model, operating system version, performance metrics. Processed anonymously via Firebase Crashlytics
Anonymous Usage App opens, screen views, feature interactions (e.g. logging a weight). Processed anonymously via Firebase Analytics

Note: We do NOT collect your personal name, email address, phone number, contacts, GPS location, or physical identity. You do not need to create an account to use all features of FitTrack.

03. How Your Data Is Stored & Protected

All fitness logs (body measurements, weight progress, calorie intake, water trackers, and workout routines) are kept directly on your mobile device in an isolated, sandboxed application database.

  • No Cloud Syncing of Fitness Data: We do not operate external servers that store your personal health numbers. Your data never leaves your device unless you choose to export or back it up yourself.
  • Data Security: The data is protected by the operating system sandbox security features built into Android and iOS. No other apps on your phone can access FitTrack's internal database without system-level tampering (root/jailbreak).

04. Third-Party Services & SDKs

To ensure app stability, detect crashes, provide advertisements, and facilitate purchases, FitTrack integrates trusted third-party software development kits (SDKs) provided by Google and Apple:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase: Used to understand general usage trends (such as which screens are popular) and improve user experience. Data collected is aggregated and anonymized.
    Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Used for real-time crash reporting and stability diagnostics to help us rapidly fix bugs. Crashlytics collects anonymous device state information and stack traces when an error occurs.
    Firebase Privacy & Security
  • Google AdMob: Used to display advertisements in the free version of the app. AdMob may process advertising identifiers to deliver relevant ads.
    Google AdMob Policies
  • Google Play In-App Billing / Apple StoreKit: Used to securely process premium upgrades (such as "Remove Ads"). Payment information is handled entirely by Google Play or Apple; FitTrack never receives or stores your credit card details.

05. Device Permissions

FitTrack may request the following device permissions only when necessary for specific features:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS / Push): Used strictly on your device to trigger daily workout and hydration reminder notifications (e.g. morning kickoff, midday check-in, evening target). You can turn notifications on or off at any time in the app Settings or in your device system settings.
  • Audio / Vibration: Used to alert you with sound or vibration when workout interval timers and rest stopwatches complete.
  • Network / Internet: Used to load exercise video guides, serve banner/interstitial ads (in the free tier), and send anonymous crash reports to Firebase.

06. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of FitTrack is supported through third-party advertising provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use pseudonymous device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID / GAID or iOS IDFA) according to Google's advertising policies.

Your Choices:

  • You can reset or opt out of personalized ads at any time via your device settings:
    • Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Delete or Reset advertising ID
    • iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking
  • You can permanently remove all advertisements by purchasing the "Remove Ads" option in the app Settings.

07. In-App Purchases & Financial Information

If you choose to purchase the "Remove Ads" lifetime upgrade or any future in-app features:

  • All financial transactions are conducted directly through the official Google Play Store (for Android) or Apple App Store (for iOS).
  • We do NOT collect, receive, or store your credit card numbers, bank account information, or billing address.
  • Your purchase receipt is linked to your Google or Apple ID account, allowing you to restore your purchase across multiple devices using the "Restore Purchases" option.

08. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

FitTrack is not intended for use by children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information through our application, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate measures to delete any such data.

09. User Rights & Data Deletion (GDPR & CCPA)

Under international privacy regulations (including GDPR and CCPA), you have the right to access, export, or completely delete your data:

  • Data Deletion: Because all your fitness and workout records are stored entirely on your device, you have full control over your data. You can delete individual measurements directly in the app, or delete all app data by going to Device Settings > Apps > FitTrack > Clear Storage / Clear Data, or by simply uninstalling the application.
  • No Residual Server Copies: When you delete data or uninstall the app, all your fitness records are permanently wiped from your device. No residual copies remain on our servers because we never stored them remotely.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in legal requirements, third-party SDK updates, or application features. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date at the top.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we are protecting your information.
